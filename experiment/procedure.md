The experiment consists of three stations, each introducing a different aspect of VR interaction: <br><br>


1.	<b>Station 1 – VR Hardware Interaction</b> <br>
o	Use the Meta Quest 3 VR controllers to grab and examine objects.<br>
o	Observe the headset’s design, tracking system, and internal components.<br><br>


2.	<b>Station 2 – Learning about VR Systems</b> <br>
o	Press a button to cycle through informational slides covering 3D modeling, game engines, and XR concepts.<br><br>

3.	<b>Station 3 – Lighting Manipulation in VR</b><br>
o	Use VR controllers to adjust a virtual lamp’s intensity and spot angle through sliders.<br>
o	Observe how changes in lighting affect the scene.<br><br>

The experiment concludes after successfully completing all three tasks.
